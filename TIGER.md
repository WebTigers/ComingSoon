# ComingSoon

**Category:** site · **Type:** module · **License:** BSD-3-Clause · **Price:** free

A branded, single-page "Coming Soon" holding page for a fresh Tiger site — professional from the moment
DNS resolves, gone the moment the real site publishes. Five preset skins (midnight, clean, bold, warm,
studio), zero-config beyond the skin, one request, no CDN. Bundled with TigerInstall as the default
fresh-install landing.

- Owns the public home (`/`) while active; every other route untouched.
- Reads its content (name, tagline, contact, social) from Site Identity — nothing to fill in.
- Owner picks a skin in the panel, with a live preview and a one-click "Go live".
